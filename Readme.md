# Microsoft 365 Teams & Outlook — Medium

Training materials and practical resources for a 3-hour Microsoft 365 training session focused on **Microsoft Outlook and Microsoft Teams**.

The course is designed for users who already have experience with Microsoft 365 and want to make better use of Outlook and Teams for everyday communication, meetings, collaboration, and productivity.

## Topics

### Microsoft Outlook

* Email and communication
* Calendar and meeting management
* Scheduling and availability
* Organizing and managing daily work
* Working with Teams meetings from Outlook

### Microsoft Teams

* Teams and channels
* Conversations and collaboration
* Meetings
* File sharing and collaboration
* Apps and productivity features
* Tips and shortcuts

### Outlook + Teams

* Connecting email, calendar, meetings, and collaboration
* Creating Teams meetings from Outlook
* Moving conversations from email into Teams
* Collaborating on files and content
* Choosing the right tool for different communication and collaboration needs

## Training

**Duration:** 3 hours
**Level:** Medium
**Applications:** Microsoft Outlook and Microsoft Teams
**Audience:** Microsoft 365 users in a professional environment

## Resources

The repository contains the material and resources used to support the training, including practical examples, demonstrations, and reference material.
