# Get Started with Microsoft Teams

A practical introduction to **Microsoft Teams** for users who are beginning to use the platform to communicate, collaborate, and organize their daily work.

## Objectives

By the end of this session, participants will be able to:

* Navigate the Microsoft Teams interface.
* Configure their profile, presence, status, and preferences.
* Manage notifications and privacy settings.
* Use individual and group chats.
* Create mentions and format messages.
* Organize teams and channels.
* Participate in conversations using threads.
* Share and manage files.
* Create, schedule, and join meetings.
* Configure audio, video, and devices for meetings.
* Share a screen or specific window during a meeting.
* Make individual calls and review call history.
* Access files used in Teams.
* Use the built-in help and training resources.
