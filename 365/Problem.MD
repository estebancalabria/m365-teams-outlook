# Microsoft 365 Integrations: Why This Course Matters

## English Version

Good morning everyone.

Before we begin, I would like you to think about a situation that is probably very familiar to most organizations today.

Your company has invested heavily in Microsoft 365.

You have Teams.

You have Outlook.

You have SharePoint.

You have OneDrive.

You have Excel, Word, and PowerPoint.

Perhaps you even have Power Platform and Microsoft 365 Copilot.

Yet despite having all these tools available, many organizations continue to face the same productivity challenges they had before.

People cannot find the information they need.

Documents exist in multiple versions.

Files are duplicated.

Information is shared through email, chat messages, folders, and links spread across different locations.

Users are often unsure where content should be stored.

They do not always understand who has access to what.

And many business processes still depend on manual work, repetitive tasks, and endless email exchanges.

So the real question is not whether we have technology.

The real question is why friction still exists.

Because in many cases, the problem is not the lack of tools.

The problem is using an integrated ecosystem as if it were a collection of disconnected applications.

And this is where the data becomes interesting.

According to Microsoft's Work Trend Index, 62% of employees say they spend too much time searching for information during their workday.

Think about that for a moment.

More than half of workers feel they waste excessive time simply trying to find what they need.

The same research shows that 57% of digital work time is spent on communication.

Meetings.

Emails.

Chat messages.

Only 43% is spent creating actual value.

In addition, 68% of workers report that they lack enough uninterrupted focus time to perform deep work.

Other studies suggest that as much as 41% of the workday can be consumed by repetitive or low-value activities.

Now, none of these statistics prove that Microsoft 365 is causing the problem.

In fact, the opposite is true.

These numbers demonstrate that organizations everywhere face digital fragmentation, information overload, and operational inefficiencies.

The question becomes: how do we address those challenges?

This is where integrations matter.

When someone shares a file in a Teams channel, they are not working only with Teams.

Behind the scenes, SharePoint is involved.

When a file is shared in a chat conversation, OneDrive is often involved.

When a Team is created, a Microsoft 365 Group is created as well.

When permissions are assigned, identity and security services operate in the background.

When a process is automated, Power Automate becomes part of the solution.

And when Copilot generates answers, analyzes documents, or summarizes conversations, it relies on all those connected systems working together.

In other words, Microsoft 365 is not a collection of applications.

It is a connected platform.

Many organizations believe that users do not use SharePoint.

But often, users are actually using SharePoint every day without realizing it.

The integrations exist.

The challenge is that they are frequently invisible to the end user.

This is why this course is not about learning buttons.

It is not about memorizing menus.

It is not about isolated features.

The goal is to understand the complete ecosystem.

Where information lives.

How data flows.

How permissions work.

How Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Outlook, Power Platform, and Copilot connect with one another.

Because once those relationships become clear, unnecessary searches decrease.

Duplicate files decrease.

Manual work decreases.

Collaboration improves.

Governance improves.

And organizations create the foundation required for Copilot and AI solutions to deliver real value.

Throughout this course, we will build that integrated mental model together.

We will connect the pieces.

We will understand what happens behind the applications we use every day.

And we will learn how to transform a collection of tools into a true digital workplace platform.
