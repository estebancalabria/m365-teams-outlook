# LAB — Understanding Microsoft 365 Groups, Outlook, Teams and SharePoint

## Objective

Understand how Microsoft 365 Groups connect Outlook, Teams and SharePoint.

---

# Part 1 — Explore Microsoft 365 Groups in Outlook

## Step 1 — Open Outlook

```text
[BROWSER] → https://outlook.cloud.microsoft/
```

---

## Step 2 — Explore Groups

```text
[BROWSER] → https://outlook.cloud.microsoft/
→ [LEFT NAVBAR] Groups
```

Observe:

```text
My Groups
```

Review the Groups available to your account.

---

## Step 3 — Explore Discover Groups

```text
[BROWSER] → https://outlook.cloud.microsoft/
→ [LEFT NAVBAR] Groups

[PAGE SECTION] Discover Groups
```

Observe:

* Public Groups
* Private Groups
* Join
* Request to Join

Notice that groups may be visible even when you are not a member.

---

# Part 2 — Explore a Microsoft 365 Group

## Step 4 — Open a Group

```text
[BROWSER] → https://outlook.cloud.microsoft/
→ [LEFT NAVBAR] Groups
→ [GROUP] Any Available Group
```

Observe the available options.

Example:

```text
Conversations
Files
Members
```

---

## Step 5 — Explore Group Conversations

```text
[BROWSER] → https://outlook.cloud.microsoft/
→ [LEFT NAVBAR] Groups
→ [GROUP] Selected Group
→ [OPTION] Conversations
```

Observe how communication occurs through Outlook group conversations.

---

# Part 3 — Explore Teams

## Step 6 — Open Microsoft Teams

```text
[BROWSER] → https://teams.microsoft.com/
```

---

## Step 7 — Open a Team

```text
[BROWSER] → https://teams.microsoft.com/
→ [LEFT NAVBAR] Teams
→ [TEAM] Select a Team
```

Observe:

```text
General
Marketing
Finance
```

or equivalent channels.

---

## Step 8 — Explore Channel Conversations

```text
[BROWSER] → https://teams.microsoft.com/
→ [TEAM] Selected Team
→ [CHANNEL] General
→ [TAB] Posts
```

Observe the discussion experience.

Compare mentally with:

```text
Outlook Group
→ Conversations
```

# Part 4 — Explore Team Files

## Step 9 — Open Files

```text
[BROWSER] → https://teams.microsoft.com/
→ [TEAM] Selected Team
→ [CHANNEL] General
→ [TAB] Files
```

Observe the files exposed through Teams.

---

## Step 10 — Open SharePoint

```text
[BROWSER] → https://teams.microsoft.com/
→ [TEAM] Selected Team
→ [CHANNEL] General
→ [TAB] Files
→ [BUTTON] Open in SharePoint
```

Observe that Teams files are stored in SharePoint.

---

# Part 5 — Explore Channel Email Addresses

## Step 11 — Obtain a Channel Email Address

```text
[BROWSER] → https://teams.microsoft.com/
→ [TEAM] Selected Team
→ [CHANNEL] General
→ [MENU] ...
→ [OPTION] Get Email Address
```

Observe the generated address.

Example:

```text
general.xxxxx@amer.teams.ms
```

Note that this is a Channel email address.

It is not the Microsoft 365 Group email address.

---

# Part 6 — Identify the Relationships

Review the following models.

## Microsoft 365 Group

```text
Microsoft 365 Group
│
├─ Outlook
├─ SharePoint
└─ Teams (optional)
```

---

## Standard Team

```text
Standard Team
│
├─ Microsoft 365 Group
└─ SharePoint Site
```

---

## Communication Options

### Group Communication

```text
Group Email
        ↓
Outlook Conversations
```

### Team Communication

```text
Teams Channel
        ↓
Posts
```

---

## Visibility

A user may:

```text
See Teams
```

without necessarily being able to discover:

```text
Group Email Address
Group Visibility in Outlook
```

The absence of a Group in Outlook does not automatically prove that the Microsoft 365 Group does not exist.

---

# Key Concepts Learned

```text
Microsoft 365 Group
├─ Outlook
├─ SharePoint
└─ Teams (optional)
```

```text
Every Standard Team
      ↓
Has a Microsoft 365 Group
```

```text
Every Microsoft 365 Group
      ↓
Has a SharePoint Site
```

```text
Not Every Microsoft 365 Group
      ↓
Has a Team
```

```text
Not Every SharePoint Site
      ↓
Has a Microsoft 365 Group
```

```text
Group Email
      ≠
Channel Email
```

```text
Outlook Conversations
      ≠
Teams Posts
```
