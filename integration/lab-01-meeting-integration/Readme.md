# LAB 1 — Create a Teams Meeting from Outlook

## Objective

In this lab, you will create a Microsoft Teams meeting using Outlook.

You will learn how to:

* Create a new calendar event in Outlook.
* Add people to the meeting.
* Add a Microsoft Teams meeting.
* Add an agenda.
* Send the meeting invitation.
* Find the meeting in Microsoft Teams.

---

## Scenario

You need to organize a meeting with your team to discuss the current status of a project.

You will create the meeting in Outlook and use Microsoft Teams to conduct the meeting online.

---

# Step 1 — Open Outlook

1. Open your web browser.

2. Go to:

   **https://outlook.office.com/**

3. Sign in with your Microsoft 365 account if required.

4. When Outlook opens, look at the navigation menu on the left side.

5. Select **Calendar**.

---

# Step 2 — Create a new event

1. In the Calendar, select **New event**.

2. A new event window will open.

3. In the **Title** field, enter:

   `Project Review`

---

# Step 3 — Set the date and time

1. Locate the **Date** field.

2. Select the date provided by your instructor.

3. Select the **Start time** provided by your instructor.

4. Select the **End time** provided by your instructor.

For example:

* **Start:** 10:00 AM
* **End:** 11:00 AM

> Use the date and time specified by your instructor rather than the example above.

---

# Step 4 — Add participants

1. Find the field where you can add people to the meeting.

2. Enter the email address of the first participant provided by your instructor.

3. Select the participant when Outlook displays the matching account.

4. Add the other participants provided by your instructor.

The participants will receive the meeting invitation when you send the event.

---

# Step 5 — Add Microsoft Teams

1. Find the **Teams meeting** option in the event window.

2. Select **Teams meeting**.

Outlook will add Microsoft Teams meeting information to the event.

The participants will receive a link that they can use to join the meeting.

---

# Step 6 — Add an agenda

Click inside the main body of the meeting invitation.

Enter the following text:

```text
Agenda

1. Project status
2. Review of pending tasks
3. Next steps
```

The complete meeting should now contain:

* A title.
* A date and time.
* Participants.
* A Microsoft Teams meeting.
* An agenda.

---

# Step 7 — Review the meeting

Before sending the invitation, review the information you entered.

Check that:

* The meeting title is **Project Review**.
* The date is correct.
* The start and end times are correct.
* All required participants have been added.
* The Teams meeting option is enabled.
* The agenda is included.

---

# Step 8 — Send the invitation

1. Select **Send**.

2. Outlook will send the meeting invitation to all participants.

3. The meeting will now appear in your Outlook calendar.

---

# Step 9 — Find the meeting in Teams

Now you will access the same meeting from Microsoft Teams.

1. Open Microsoft Teams.

2. In the left navigation menu, select **Calendar**.

3. Find the meeting called:

   **Project Review**

4. Select the meeting.

You should see the same meeting that you created in Outlook.

---

# Step 10 — Review the Teams meeting

With the meeting open in Teams, look for:

* The meeting title.
* The date and time.
* The participants.
* The option to join the meeting.

The meeting created in Outlook is now available in Teams.

---

## Lab completed

You have created a Microsoft Teams meeting from Outlook and accessed the same meeting from Teams.

**Outlook** is used to organize the calendar event, while **Teams** provides the online meeting experience.
